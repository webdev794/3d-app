Export from CorelDRAW first

This file is a CorelDRAW file (.cdr), which can't be opened here. Save a copy from CorelDRAW as SVG or PNG, then import that.

  1. In CorelDRAW, open the design and choose File › Export (Ctrl+E).
  2. Set Save as type to SVG to keep shapes and text editable here, or PNG for a picture (tick Transparent background for logos).
  3. Click Choose SVG or PNG below and pick the exported file.

Text looks different after import? In CorelDRAW, convert the text to curves (Ctrl+Q) before exporting.

Tools and shortcuts

Tools

Pick V · Shape (nodes) F10 · Freehand F5 · Rectangle F6 · Ellipse F7 · Text F8 · Polygon Y · Star S · Graph paper D · Spiral A · Eraser X · Zoom Z · Pan H · Interactive fill G · Mesh fill M · Eyedropper I (click a colour, then click objects to fill them)

Artistic Media (Preset, Brush, Sprayer, Calligraphic, Expression; Painterly Brush and Variable Outline in its flyout) is the third button, below Pan. Smart Drawing and LiveSketch are in the Freehand flyout. A button with a small triangle has a flyout: click the button again, click the triangle or right-click it (on a phone, hold a finger on it); the button then shows the tool you used last. The Rectangle flyout has every shape tool: Rectangle, 3-Point Rectangle, Ellipse, 3-Point Ellipse, Polygon, Star, Complex Star, Graph Paper, Spiral, Impact and the basic, arrow, banner, callout and badge shapes. Interactive Fill, Mesh Fill and Smart Fill share one flyout; Contour, Blend, Drop Shadow, Inner Shadow, Block Shadow, Extrude, Distort and Envelope share another (Transparency and the Eyedropper have their own buttons). The other flyouts: Freehand Pick, Free Transform, Smooth, Smear, Twirl, Attract, Repel, Smudge, Roughen, Knife, Virtual Segment Delete, Bézier, Pen, B-Spline, Polyline, 3-Point Curve, Paragraph Text, dimensions (parallel, horizontal or vertical, angular, segment) and the 2-Leg Callout, connectors (straight, right-angle, rounded right-angle) and Anchor Editing.

Curves and nodes

Convert to curves Ctrl+Q · Outline to object Ctrl+Shift+Q · Combine Ctrl+L · Break apart Ctrl+K

Shape tool: drag nodes, handles or segments · double-click a curve to add a node, a node to delete it · drag a box to select nodes · Shift+click adds · arrows nudge · Alt+drag a handle breaks symmetry.

Bézier / Pen: click for corner nodes, click-drag for smooth nodes, click the first node to close.

Arrange

Size, rotate, slant (Pick): one click on an object shows its 6 sizing handles: the corners resize, the left and right middles stretch the width (the height alone: the H box with Keep proportions off, or Shift + a corner). Click it again for the rotate and slant handles: drag a curved corner arrow to rotate (Ctrl or Shift: 15° steps), a side arrow to slant, the circle to move the centre of rotation; click again for the sizing handles. Dragging the object moves it in both. Double-click a text to type in it.
