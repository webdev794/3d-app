Drag-and-drop for the pattern thumbnails (website + app):
- Users can drag a pattern from the glass panel onto the garment.
- Mobile: press and hold a thumbnail to start dragging. Desktop: click and drag.
- While dragging, minimise the panel temporarily so the garment is easy to reach.
- Highlight the garment area under the pointer/finger (front, back, sleeve...).
- On release, apply the pattern to that highlighted area and restore the panel.
- Dropping outside the garment cancels.
- Keep tap-to-apply (selected garment area or Whole garment setting).
- Undo reverses the change.
